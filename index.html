Wrong light picked? Tap the moon on the screen.

Or use the iPhone Camera

  • Get trees, rooftops or a skyline in the frame. The foreground is what makes the moon feel big.
  • Use 2x, 3x or 5x zoom if your iPhone has it. The app enlarges more from there.
  • Touch and hold the moon on screen to lock focus, then drag the ☀︎ slider down until the moon stops being a white blob.
  • Lean on something steady and take 3–5 shots in a row. Picking them all lets the app merge them, which removes the most grain.
